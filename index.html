How buildable % is figured

Buildable % is the share of the parcel left after excluding: open water, wetlands, FEMA flood zones A/AE and floodway, slopes of 20% or more, stream buffers (100 ft on perennial/named streams, 50 ft on others), setbacks (50 ft front / 25 ft side-rear outside Greenville County; Greenville uses its zoning setbacks), and a 25 ft buffer on Lakes Keowee/Jocassee. No easement or cemetery data is included. The septic screen uses USDA soil maps only; only an SC DES site evaluation (about $150) is definitive. Screening only, not a survey.

LandWatch and Realtor.com were not searched (they block automated access); some other source sites have not been searched yet. Septic, slope and flood values are single-point checks at the listing's map location, not a % of the parcel. Rows marked 'approx. location' were placed from the town center, not the street; tax-sale parcels from the county seat. Always verify on the listing and county GIS.
